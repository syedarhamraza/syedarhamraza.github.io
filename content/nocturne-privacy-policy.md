# Nocturne Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Nocturne is a watch face for Wear OS and a companion app for Android phones, made by Syed Arham Raza. This policy explains what they collect and why.

## Nothing is collected

Nocturne has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Neither the watch face nor the phone app sends any data to the developer or to anyone else.

## On your watch

The watch face shows data your watch already has: the time and date, your battery level and the complications you choose. Wear OS gives this data to the face on your watch. The face doesn't store it, copy it or send it anywhere. Your choices in **Customise** (dial, metal, logo, hour markers, subdials and digital time) are kept by Wear OS on the watch.

## On your phone

The phone app finds the watches paired with your phone and, when you tap **Install on watch**, asks Google Play services to open Nocturne's Play Store page on your watch. What it learns about your watch (its name and whether it's connected) never leaves your phone.

**Software update** asks Google Play whether a newer version of the app is available, and downloads and installs it only when you tap the button. If you turn on **Auto download**, the app asks Google Play once when you open it; to honour "Using Wi-Fi only", it checks whether your phone is on Wi-Fi. That setting is saved on your phone. The app doesn't connect to the internet itself: Google Play does the checking and downloading.

The **Customise preview** and the themes are images inside the app; nothing is downloaded to show them.

Google Play services and the Play Store handle installs and updates under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

Nocturne isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

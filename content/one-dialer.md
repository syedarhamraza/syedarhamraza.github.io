# One Dialer

One Dialer is a phone and contacts app for Android, with a calm, glassy design.

## Get the official version

One Dialer is in **closed beta on Google Play**. To join, email [arhamraza399@gmail.com](mailto:arhamraza399@gmail.com?subject=One%20Dialer%20beta) with the Google account you use on your phone. You'll get a link to install it from Google Play.

## Got it as an APK file?

Copies shared as APK files, or pulled out of a phone by another app, aren't official. They don't get updates and may have been changed. Uninstall it and install One Dialer from Google Play instead.
